# CHAIR-002 — THE INDEPENDENT SECOND CHAIR

**Standing: OPEN / UNASSIGNED / NO INVITATION SENT.**

This is a **place to volunteer**, not an instruction to any named person or partner. Opening this file does not recruit anyone, grant access or certify independence.

## What a willing second adult could do

Review whether the founding steward's process is fair, whether the rules make disagreement safe, and whether future complaints involving the founder are handled by a person who has no material conflict of interest.

The work is initially modest: a **20-minute review of fictional scenarios** and the public [First Chair Declaration](FIRST-CHAIR-001.md). No member database access, operator passwords, private complaints or survivor testimony are required.

A prospective reviewer can decline, question the draft, propose a change, or offer to examine one bounded issue later. None of those acts automatically appoints them to the role.

## Minimum steps before any real appointment

1. The person opts in themselves, understands the limited scope and agrees how their independence will be assessed for each particular decision.
2. Check conflicts of interest before relying on a review. Another membership credential or a friendly relationship is not sufficient to prove independent judgment.
3. Establish separate custody for the reviewer's notes and any permitted evidence; require source-specific permission and secure handling.
4. Agree an appeals, recusal and conflict-response method that does **not** let a challenged steward veto review or sanction dissent.
5. Log a bounded decision with its uncertainties and the reviewer's separately expressed approval. A process concern never becomes a finding of misconduct merely by being filed.
6. If no suitable independent person is available, leave the question pending rather than pretending the founder can self-certify.

## Suggested initial invitation wording

> We're testing a volunteer-run community accountability process. Would you be willing to spend 20 minutes examining the public rules and a fictional example? You wouldn't receive anyone's private information or need to agree to an ongoing role. The goal is to discover what the first steward might be missing. Saying no is entirely fine.

This is a **draft invitation**, not a sent communication.

## No fabricated membership

CHAIR-002 currently has **no assignee, no verified consent, no audit credentials, no voting power and no completed review**. An actual second chair should have a separate human approval record and a bounded, revocable authority grant, not just a published name.

**Watchman 001 can begin practicing accountable behavior before Watchman 002 exists. Watchman 001 cannot claim independent validation before someone independent acts.**
