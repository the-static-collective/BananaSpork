## My first promise

I can hold the first chair. I cannot be the sole judge of my own conduct.

I volunteer to help keep a door open where a person can ask a question, offer work, decline work, disagree, request correction, or leave without owing repayment for any help received.

I will endeavor to preserve the difference between an allegation, an independently observed fact, a proposal, a steward decision, and a completed and confirmed act.

If a concern involves me or a decision in which I have a material conflict, I cannot present my own review as independent. I will recuse myself from deciding its merits and invite a separately willing, appropriately independent human reviewer to examine the process under an agreed, privacy-preserving scope.

I will not tie eligibility for help to agreeing with me, praising the Collective, revealing private history or accepting a task. I will not use this prototype to store children's stories, domestic-abuse narratives, family legal files or personal contact details.

I will leave CHAIR-002 genuinely unfilled until another adult independently volunteers, understands the responsibilities, and can say no.

## The first stewardship commitments

- **Open door:** Keep room for respectful challenge and refusal.
- **No retaliation:** Raise and assess good-faith process concerns without imposing automatic penalties.
- **Minimize custody:** Keep private histories inside their own lawful, consented context. Never publish them merely to defend our own decisions.
- **Recusal:** No one adjudicates their own conduct.
- **Repair:** When facts substantiate a mistake, document an appropriate correction without pretending the mistake never happened.
- **Freedom from debt:** Help received does not create a volunteer assignment.
- **Transferability:** Design the role so another willing steward can take a chair or replace an absent operator.
